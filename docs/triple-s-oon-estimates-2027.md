## How to read it

- Reimbursement is based on **Triple-S's contracted rate, not what the patient paid**: roughly
  *average tariff − copay − coinsurance*. Specialist: $32 − $15 = $17. A patient who paid a cash specialist
  $150 gets back about $17 and owes the rest.
- It applies only where the product **includes a reimbursement benefit** (or the service is an emergency).
  The five 2027 individual plans we read don't have a general one (see
  [triple-s-2027-reembolsos.md](triple-s-2027-reembolsos.md)); the PYMES (small/medium business) products may.
- Averages, not quotes.

- MCS publishes a Ley 134-2020 cost estimator: https://mcs.com.pr/en/Pages/No-Surprise-Act-Cost-Calculator.aspx
- First Medical sells an individual **"Pago Directo Oro PPO"** plan whose benefits description has a section
  "Reembolso de gastos cubiertos por servicios obtenidos fuera de la red" (2025 document on docs.pr.gov).
- OCS lists ACA-compliant insurers selling 2027 plans in PR: First Medical, MCS Life, Plan de Salud Menonita,
  Plan Médico Bella Vista, Triple-S, Ryder Health Plan. Plan documents are hosted under
  `docs.pr.gov/files/OCS/Seguros_Salud/Planes en Cumplimiento/<year>/`.
