## Bottom line

**None of these plans reimburses a cash-pay, non-participating provider in Puerto Rico for routine
care.** "POS" here means two in-network tiers, not out-of-network coverage. Every plan has:

- **Exclusion #52** — services by non-participating providers in PR are excluded, except (a) adult
  glasses/contacts, (b) emergencies, (c) air ambulance, (d) a non-participating provider at a
  participating facility without the patient's consent (surprise billing).
- **Exclusion #4** — any reimbursement request for a service "que no esté expresamente indicado en esta
  póliza como servicio con derecho a reembolso" is excluded.
- **§1.11/1.12** — non-participating providers are covered "únicamente en casos de emergencia, incluyendo
  ambulancia aérea, o según requerido por ley"; otherwise "usted es responsable del costo total".
- SBC out-of-network column: "No cubierto" for everything except ER, urgent care and ambulance.

Not member reimbursements, but related:

- **Emergencies (PR or US)** are paid directly to the provider at the participating rate (US: greatest of
  network rate, UCR or Medicare). No balance billing.
- **US non-emergency care** is covered only with preauthorization for services not available in PR,
  paid via BlueCard. Without preauth it is "no será elegible para reembolso".
- **Outside PR and the US**: excluded except US emergencies (exclusion #38).

## How amounts are calculated

- Copays and coinsurance are never reimbursable (definitions).
- Out-of-network spending does not count toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
- Outside emergencies, the patient owes the difference between the allowed amount and the billed charge.
- Fixed-dollar benefits (vision, ambulance, Contigo Fit, Mi Momento) are flat caps.

## Labs

No general out-of-network lab reimbursement.

- Non-participating labs in PR fall under exclusion #52; labs are never named as reimbursable (#4).
- **"Triple-S Salud no reembolsará a ningún asegurado las pruebas que no tengan orden médica"** sits under
  **Ley 29-2024**, which lets patients order certain tests themselves (glucose, CBC, CMP, A1c, lipids,
  thyroid, B12, vitamin D, CRP, STI panels, etc.) *at their own cost*. It means self-ordered tests are
  never reimbursed. It does **not** imply ordered tests from an out-of-network lab are.
- Excluded outright: tests not coded in Triple-S's Laboratory Manual or experimental (#3), heavy-metal
  testing (#53). Genetic tests need preauthorization.
- Narrow exceptions: labs inside an emergency or post-stabilization episode; clause 18/19 when no
  participating lab can perform a covered test; preauthorized US labs for tests unavailable in PR
  (paid via BlueCard).
- In-network lab coinsurance: Directo Bronce 60%, Directo Plata 35%, Pocket Plata 30%, Pocket Oro 30%,
  Uno Plata $0 (Blue One) / 40% (Blue Select). SALUS clinics $0 (confirmed for Pocket Oro).

**Practical path:** provider writes the order, patient goes to a participating lab or SALUS clinic.

## Other exclusions relevant to cash-pay practices

- Alternative therapies incl. acupuncture (#5) — except **Triple-S Natural** (acupuncture, therapeutic
  massage, naturopathic medicine, music therapy): up to 6 services/year, $15 copay, **participating
  providers only**. Medical cannabis excluded.
- Non-MD/DDS providers (#48), except audiologists, optometrists, podiatrists, psychologists, social
  workers, chiropractors and others the policy names.
- Sports medicine, psychoanalysis, cardiac rehab (#6); OT and speech therapy with narrow exceptions (#9);
  psychometric tests except autism/Down syndrome (#10); thermography (#51); weight-loss and anti-aging
  programs (#47); not medically necessary / experimental (#59/#60).
- Pharmacy: oral vitamins, supplements, homeopathic and alternative-medicine products.
- Nutrition only from PR physicians specializing in nutrition or metabolic disease.
- Chiropractic + PT capped at 20 per year combined.
- Telemedicine is paid like in-person under Ley 8-2025, but only from participating providers.

## Claim rules (all 5 plans, §5.01)

**Deadline:** written notice within 20 days of service or as soon as reasonably possible; **no later than
1 year** from the date of service unless filing was impossible. Triple-S acknowledges mailed claims
within 15 days and resolves within 30 days.

**Required for medical claims:**

1. Full name (both surnames) and contract number
2. Date of service
3. ICD-10 code and/or diagnosis
4. Procedure code (current as of the service date) and/or description
5. Provider NPI
6. Stamped payment receipt ("recibo de pago timbrado") with provider name, address, **specialty** and
   **license number**
7. Amount paid per service
8. Provider signature
9. **Reason for the reimbursement request**
10. Copy of the preauthorization, if one was required

**Where to submit:**

- Email: **reembolso@ssspr.com** (dental: reemdental@ssspr.com)
- Mi Triple-S app
- ssspr.com → Herramientas para Ti → Formularios para Persona Asegurada
- Service centers; phone 1-800-981-3241 / 787-774-6060
- Mail: Triple-S Salud, PO Box 363628, San Juan, PR 00936-3628

**Appeals:** PO Box 11320, San Juan, PR 00922-9905, qacomercial@ssspr.com.

None of the documents names the "Solicitud de Reembolso de Gastos Médicos por el Suscriptor" form; they
point to the forms section of ssspr.com.

## Implications for Aura Curativa

1. **Add a "razón de la solicitud" field.** It's the one required item the packet doesn't produce yet.
2. **Plan + route checker.** Before building a claim, tell the patient which route applies (or that none
   does) for their plan and service. Honest "not reimbursable" beats a denied claim.
3. **Clause 18/19 letter.** The "no network provider" route pays 100% and is the best fit for specialists
   Triple-S can't place; generate the request to Triple-S before the service.
4. **Contigo Fit claims** for acupuncture/massage/reflexology.
5. **Email submission** to reembolso@ssspr.com as an alternative to mail.
6. **Labs:** route to in-network labs with an order instead of filing.
7. **Open question:** these are individual-market plans. Employer group plans and mainland PPOs often do
   cover out-of-network care — confirm which plans target patients actually carry.

Not legal or insurance advice. Policies change yearly; re-check each plan year.
