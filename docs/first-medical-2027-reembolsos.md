## Plans

First Medical Directo **Gold Plus, Gold, Silver Plus, Silver** 2027. The OCS folders call them "Pago Directo
Oro / Oro Plus / Plata / Plata Plus PPO"; the documents themselves never say PPO. Network: International
Medical Card, "ambiente de LIBRE SELECCIÓN". Metro Pavía facilities are a cheaper tier inside it.

## Out-of-network reimbursement

- **Yes, generally — not only emergencies.** "Reembolsará al suscriptor el 100% de los gastos cubiertos que
  sean médicamente necesarios" (p34–35). Claim section: "Reembolso de gastos cubiertos por servicios obtenidos
  fuera de la red" (p52).
- **Amount:** the highest rate First Medical contracts with a provider of the same specialty, minus the copay
  or coinsurance, "independientemente lo que dicho proveedor fuera de la red le hubiese cobrado" (p17, p34–35).
  Example in the policy: $350 contracted rate, 30% coinsurance → $245.
- **No network provider of that specialty:** 100% of what was paid, minus copay (p35).
- **Patient pays the gap** above the allowed amount. Balance-billing protection covers emergencies and
  inadvertent out-of-network charges only (p37, p51).
- **Fee schedule not published.** Members can call **1-888-318-0274** (7 days, 8am–12am); the plan must give
  the allowed amount for a specific CPT code on request (p36).
- The lab example on p36 applies coinsurance to the billed amount, which contradicts the rule above.

## Exclusions that matter for cash-pay providers

Acupuncture (#19); sports medicine, natural medicine, music therapy, psychoanalysis, cardiac rehab (#20);
non-MD professionals other than audiologists, optometrists, podiatrists, psychologists, chiropractors (#6).

## Filing

- **Deadline: 90 days** from the date of service, or as soon as reasonably possible (p52).
- **Documents:** original receipt with the provider's printed name, address and phone; patient name and age;
  contract number; diagnosis and ICD-10; CPT codes with each charge; date the condition began; provider NPI
  (p35, p52–53).
- **Decision in 30 days;** 45 days to request reconsideration (p53).
- **Where:** the policy only says "a las oficinas del Plan". No claims address or email is published.
  General contacts: 787-474-3999, PO Box 191580, San Juan PR 00918-1580.
- **Form:** one per patient, original receipt per provider, checkbox "Proveedor no acepta el plan". The
  website has a newer version than the team's copy:
  [FM-COM_CS_23.30_03 (approved 02/20/2023)](https://www.firstmedicalpr.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/FM-COM_CS_23.30_03_S_Solicitud-de-Reembolso_Spanish_Approved_02202023.pdf).
